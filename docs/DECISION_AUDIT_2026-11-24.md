## Safe application instructions for a future website source import

1. Preserve original source and an immutable backup.
2. Match each site tracker entry to a stable decision ID and textual meaning, not only its displayed position.
3. Mark fully resolved items as resolved, and copy established answers into active canon pages.
4. Rewrite partially resolved entries to ask **only** the remaining question.
5. Keep the truly open items open.
6. Archive duplicates and superseded entries **after** confirming their answers are retained.
7. Derive the counter from actual outstanding decision records; don't hard-code the historic `35`.
8. Rebuild/test and verify the deployed site before claiming its tracker was updated.
